# PRIORITY 11: WINNING PRODUCT INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM

## Objective

Build a proprietary intelligence layer that identifies winning products, winning offers, winning creatives, and winning customer segments automatically.

The goal is to make Products4ThePeople a data-driven product discovery and scaling platform rather than simply an ecommerce store.

---

# OVERVIEW

Every customer interaction should generate actionable business intelligence.

The system should continuously answer:

* What products are winning?
* Why are they winning?
* Which audiences buy them?
* Which creatives convert best?
* Which storefronts perform best?
* Which bundles increase AOV?

# WINNING PRODUCTS DASHBOARD

## Product Score Algorithm

Each product receives a score based on:

* Conversion Rate
* Add To Cart Rate
* Revenue
* Profit Margin
* Refund Rate
* Repeat Purchases

Example:

Winning Score:
0–100

Categories:

90–100:
Scale Aggressively

70–89:
Promising

50–69:
Monitor

Below 50:
Replace or Remove
