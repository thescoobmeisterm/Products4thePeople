# DropShip Scout Daily Brief
**Date/Time (ET):** 2026-06-01 08:16
**Store Focus:** products4thepeople.com + niche satellites (personal care, fitness, pets, adjacent)

## Method
### Weighted Score (0–100)
- Demand Signal (30)
- Margin Potential (20)
- Competition Intensity, inverted (15)
- Logistics Simplicity (15)
- Creative Angle/UGC Potential (10)
- Repeat Purchase / LTV Potential (10)

### Hard Filters (must pass)
- Estimated gross margin >= 60%
- COGS + shipping <= 40% of target AOV
- Delivered package weight <= 1.2 kg
- Supplier processing <= 72h
- No obvious restricted claims/compliance traps (medical/regulated)
- At least 2 differentiated creatives possible in first 48h

### Supplier Confidence Scale
- **A:** Proven fulfillment consistency + low defect risk + reliable tracking
- **B:** Good baseline reliability with moderate variance
- **C:** Usable but needs strict QA/order throttling
